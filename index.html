Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions.

Do small businesses need GDPR procedures?

Yes. Organisations processing personal data must comply with applicable GDPR requirements. The measures needed depend on the processing activities and the associated risks.

Do we need a Data Protection Officer?

Not every organisation is required to appoint a Data Protection Officer. The legal requirements depend on its circumstances, but assigning internal privacy responsibilities can still be valuable.

Can we start with one specific issue?

Absolutely. A focused project, such as reviewing data processing agreements or conducting a risk assessment, can be a sensible first step.

Do you provide ISO 27001 certification?

Complynexa is not an accredited certification body. Instead, we help you prepare for certification, conducting gap assessments, building your ISMS documentation and controls, and getting you audit-ready for an independent accredited auditor.
